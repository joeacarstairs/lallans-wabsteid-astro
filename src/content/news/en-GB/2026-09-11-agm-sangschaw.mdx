## Sangschaw 2027 Rules

- Entries by 31st Januar 2027.
- Poetry no more than 45 lines.
- Prose no more than 3000 words.
- Translations: poetry 45 lines, prose 3000 words, text of original to be
  included.
- Submissions all in Scots (unless there's a good reason for using another language
  e.g. coup de grace, vae victis). 
- Writings previously published, accepted for publication or submitted elsewhere are not elibile.
- Submissions are judged anonymously, no no names on submissions, but name,
  address and title of submission/s on a separate page. Multiple
  submissions on separate pages, please.
- £5 per entry, £12 for 3 submissions, peyed by BACS/Paypal/cheque/cash or any
  other means to the SLS account. Proof of payment should be sent with
  submissions.

Feedback is given on every submission. Three tassies are awarded: the Hugh
MacDiarmid Tassie for poetry, the Robert McLellan Tassie for prose, and the John
MacPhail Law Tassie for translation, with £100 for 1st prize, £50 for runners-up,
in all categories.

Submissions may be sent online to the Society, or to failte@go-plus.net, or
as paper copies to Sangschaw, c/o 6 Dryden Place, Edinburgh EH9 1RP.

All submissions will be considered for publication in Lallans magazine.
