---
title: William and David Hershaw Live at the Edinburgh Fringe
summary: >-
  An hour of contemporary Scots folk music with two award-winning songwriters, including our own
  Wullie Hershaw.
---

Our editor, Wullie Hershaw, is performing with his son, David, at the Edinburgh Fringe. He'll be
there for one night only, Saturday 16 August, 9pm, at the Ukrainian Community Centre, 14 Royal
Terrace, EH7 5AB. Tickets will cost £12 or £10 concession.
