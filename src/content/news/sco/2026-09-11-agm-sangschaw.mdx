The agenda as it currently staunds is ablo.

Meanwhile, the Sangschaw is apen for 2027! See the rules ablo.

2. Maitters arisin
3. Preses’ Report
4. Thesaurer’s Report
5. Lallans

7. Election o Office Bearers: Preses, Vice-Preses, Secretar, Admin & Membership
   Secretar, Thesaurer

Memmers: keep an ee on your emails for the Zoom link!

## Sangschaw 2027 Rules

- Entries by 31st Januar 2027.
- Poesie nae mair nor 45 lines.
- Prose nae mair nor 3000 words.
- Owersettins: poesie 45 lines, prose 3000 words, text o oreiginal tae be
  includit.
- Submeissions aa in Scots (sauf gin there's guid reason for yaisin anither leid
  e.g. coup de grace, vae victis). 
- Screivins aareddies published, acceptit for publication or submittit
  itherwhaur are no eligible.
- Submeissions are judged anonymously, sae nae nems on submeissions, but nem,
  address an title of submeission/submeissions on a separate page. Multiple
  submeissions on separate pages, please.
- £5 per entry, £12 for 3 submeissions, peyed by BACS/Paypal/cheque/cash or ony
  ither means tae the SLS account. Pruif o peyment shuid be send wi
  submeissions.

Feedback is gien on ilka submeission. Three tassies are awardit: the Hugh
MacDiarmid Tassie for poesie, the Robert McLellan Tassie for prose, an the John
MacPhail Law Tassie for owersettin, wi £100 for 1st prize, £50 for rinners-up,
in aa categories.

Submeissions may be sent online tae the Associe, or tae failte@go-plus.net, or
as paper copies tae Sangschaw, c/o 6 Dryden Place, Edinburgh EH9 1RP.

Aa submeissions wull be considered for furthsettin i Lallans magazine.
