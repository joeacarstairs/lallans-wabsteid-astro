title: William and David Hershaw Live at the Edinburgh Fringe
summary: >-
  An hour o contemporar Scots folk music wi twa award-winnin sangscrievers, includin our ain Wullie
  Hershaw.

Our eiditor, Wullie Hershaw, is performing wi his son, David, at the Edinburgh Fringe. He'll be
there for ae nicht anely, Saturday 16 August, 9pm, at the Ukrainian Community Centre, 14 Royal
Terrace, EH7 5AB. Tickets will cost £12 or £10 concession.
